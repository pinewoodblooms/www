Ordering Notes

Terms, Conditions & Care

Please review these details before ordering. Because every piece is handmade, final timelines, delivery options, and custom details are confirmed when your order is quoted.

Pinewood Blooms creates handcrafted wax floral arrangements, decorative vases, and nature-inspired keepsakes. Product photos, colors, foliage placement, vase details, and overall scale may vary slightly because each piece is made by hand and materials are sourced in small batches. We may update products, pricing, availability, site content, or policies at any time.

All Pinewood Blooms logos, product images, descriptions, arrangement designs, website copy, graphics, and branding are the property of Pinewood Blooms unless otherwise noted. Please do not copy, reproduce, resell, or use our photos, designs, or written content for commercial purposes without written permission.

Pinewood Blooms arrangements are decorative items and are not food, toys, or fresh flowers. Keep arrangements away from open flame, direct heat, pets, and small children. Do not burn an arrangement unless a product is clearly labeled for candle use, and always follow the care guidance included with your order.

Arrangements may include wax florals, faux greenery, dried or preserved botanicals, fragrance, ribbon, ceramic vases or containers, and other decorative materials. If you have sensitivities to fragrance, botanicals, waxes, dyes, or related materials, please contact us before ordering. Store pieces in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight, water, humidity, and high heat to help preserve color, shape, and texture.

Ready-made items are typically prepared within 1-3 business days. Custom arrangements usually require 1-4 weeks depending on design complexity, materials, order size, and season. Shipping, local delivery, or pickup options are confirmed during checkout or quoting. Carrier timelines are estimates and may change due to weather, holidays, or delays outside our control.

Because each item is handmade and many orders are custom or gift-prepared, sales are generally final once production begins or an item ships. If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from the confirmed order details, please contact us promptly with your order information and clear photos so we can review the issue.

Pinewood Blooms works in small batches, so product availability, vases, containers, botanicals, and seasonal colors may be limited. Discounts, market specials, custom quotes, and promotional offers cannot be combined unless we confirm otherwise in writing.

Wax is sensitive to heat. During warm weather, arrangements may soften if exposed to high temperatures in transit, mailboxes, vehicles, porches, or direct sun. Bring packages indoors quickly and allow them to return to room temperature before handling. Heat-related softening after delivery is outside our control.

If something is wrong with your order, email pinewoodblooms@gmail.com with your name, order details, delivery date, and photos. We review order concerns individually and will respond with the next available resolution.
