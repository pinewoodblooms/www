Timing

How long does a custom arrangement take?

Small arrangements are typically ready after 3-5 available days, medium arrangements after 5-7 available days, and large arrangements after 7-10 available days. Blocked-out dates and larger quantities may extend timing.

Local orders

Do you offer pickup or delivery?

Terms, Conditions & Care

Please review these details before ordering. Because every piece is handmade, final pickup or local delivery timing, sales tax, and custom details are confirmed when your order is reviewed.

Pinewood Blooms creates handcrafted scented wax floral arrangements, decorative vases, and nature-inspired keepsakes. Product photos, colors, fragrance, foliage placement, vase details, and overall scale may vary slightly because each piece is made by hand and materials are sourced in small batches. Custom order prices are listed before applicable NY sales tax, which is added when your order is confirmed. We may update products, pricing, availability, site content, or policies at any time.

All Pinewood Blooms logos, product images, descriptions, arrangement designs, website copy, graphics, and branding are the property of Pinewood Blooms unless otherwise noted. Please do not copy, reproduce, resell, or use our photos, designs, or written content for commercial purposes without written permission.

Pinewood Blooms arrangements are decorative items and are not food, toys, or fresh flowers. Keep arrangements away from open flame, direct heat, pets, and small children. Do not burn an arrangement unless a product is clearly labeled for candle use, and always follow the care guidance included with your order.

Arrangements may include wax florals, faux greenery, dried or preserved botanicals, fragrance, ribbon, ceramic vases or containers, and other decorative materials. If you have sensitivities to fragrance, botanicals, waxes, dyes, or related materials, please contact us before ordering. Store pieces in a cool, dry area away from direct sunlight, water, humidity, and high heat to help preserve color, shape, and texture.

Custom order timing is based on the size and quantity selected in the order form. Small vases with 6-8 flowers are typically ready after 3-5 available days. Medium vases with 10-12 flowers are typically ready after 5-7 available days. Large vases with 16-18 flowers are typically ready after 7-10 available days. The calendar blocks the current day and next preparation days before pickup or local delivery can be selected. Quantities of 5 or more add 3 available days to the standard preparation window. Orders over 10 may impact the requested pickup or local delivery date, and Pinewood Blooms will reach out to confirm timing. Blocked-out dates, holidays, material availability, larger quantities, or seasonal volume may extend the earliest pickup or local delivery date. Larger venue, wedding, or event orders may not fit the standard order timeframe and will be quoted separately based on quantity, style, materials, and requested date. In-person pickup is arranged directly with Pinewood Blooms. Local delivery requests are reviewed by location and availability before the order is confirmed.

Small custom vases are $35 before applicable NY sales tax. Medium custom vases are $45 before applicable NY sales tax. Large custom vases are $65 before applicable NY sales tax. The form provides an estimated pre-tax total based on size and quantity for standard small, medium, and large orders. Quantities over 10 and larger venue, wedding, or event orders are confirmed or quoted separately. Final sales tax, pickup details, local delivery availability, and any requested changes are confirmed before the order is accepted.

Because each item is handmade and many orders are custom or gift-prepared, sales are generally final once production begins. Please review your arrangement at pickup or local drop-off so any final questions can be discussed in person.

Pinewood Blooms works in small batches, so product availability, vases, containers, botanicals, and seasonal colors may be limited. Discounts, market specials, custom quotes, and promotional offers cannot be combined unless we confirm otherwise in writing.

Wax is sensitive to heat. During warm weather, arrangements may soften if exposed to high temperatures in vehicles, porches, direct sun, or other warm areas. Keep arrangements cool during pickup or local delivery, bring them indoors quickly, and allow them to return to room temperature before handling. Heat-related softening after pickup or delivery is outside our control.
